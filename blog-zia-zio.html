Meet Zia & Zio: The Cosmic Cat Characters

May 2026 · 3 min read

Who Are Zia & Zio?

Zia and Zio are the star characters of Pawfect Match. Zia is a pink-haired cosmic cat with a playful personality, while Zio is a blue-grey cat with piercing golden eyes. They come in multiple unique variants — each with their own look and style.

How to Unlock Them

You can unlock Zia and Zio characters in two ways:

  • ⭐ 500 Stars — spend stars earned from gameplay to unlock a random character.
  • 💳 $0.99 — unlock instantly via Stripe or PayPal payment.

Where to View Your Collection

Head to the Collection tab in the main menu to see all your unlocked characters. Locked characters appear with a 🔒 icon. Tap "View Collection" in the Shop to browse what's available before you unlock.
