5 Ways to Earn More Stars in Pawfect Match

May 2026 · 4 min read

1. Claim Your Daily Bonus Every Day

The easiest 100 ⭐ you'll ever earn. Visit the Shop section every day and tap "Claim 100 ⭐". It resets every 24 hours — don't miss it. Over a month that's 3,000 free stars just for logging in.

2. Match Tiles for Maximum Points

Every tile you remove from the board gives you 10 ⭐, but matching three identical tiles gives you a bonus 300 ⭐. Focus on creating matches rather than just removing tiles. Chain matches together for big star hauls.

3. Complete Levels Without Using Power-ups

Power-ups cost stars to use. If you can clear a level without using Undo or Shuffle, you keep all the stars you earned. Save power-ups for harder levels where you really need them.

4. Replay Early Levels

Levels 1–3 are quick and easy. If you need stars fast, replay them. Each completed level earns you stars from tile matches. It's not glamorous but it works when you need a quick boost.

5. Plan Your Moves Carefully

The more efficiently you clear tiles, the more stars you earn per level. Look ahead — identify clusters of the same tile and work toward matching them systematically. Rushing leads to dead ends and wasted moves.
