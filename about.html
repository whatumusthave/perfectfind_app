About Pawfect Match

Pawfect Match is a cosmic cat puzzle adventure game created for players who love satisfying tile-matching challenges. Whether you're a casual gamer looking for a relaxing experience or a puzzle enthusiast chasing high scores, Pawfect Match has something for everyone.

Meet Zia & Zio

At the heart of Pawfect Match are our beloved characters — Zia, the charming pink cat, and Zio, the mysterious blue cat. Together they guide you through 10 increasingly challenging levels filled with cosmic cat artifacts, rare collectibles, and celestial mysteries.

How to Play

Select tiles from the board and collect them in your slot bar. Match 3 identical tiles to clear them and earn star points. Clear all tiles before your slot bar fills up to achieve the Pawfect Match! Use Undo, Shuffle, and daily bonuses to help you through tough levels.

Our Mission

We believe games should be beautiful, satisfying, and accessible. Pawfect Match is designed to be free to play with optional upgrades that enhance — not gate — your experience. Play at your own pace, collect rare card skins, and unlock Zia & Zio companions as you master each level.
