How to Play Pawfect Match: Beginner's Guide

May 2026 · 5 min read

What is Pawfect Match?

Pawfect Match is a tile-matching puzzle game where you select cards from a stacked board and try to match three identical cards in your collection bar. Clear all tiles from the board before the bar fills up to win each stage.

Step 1: Select Tiles

Tap any accessible tile on the board to move it into your collection bar at the bottom. Only tiles that are not covered by other tiles can be selected. Look for tiles on the top layer of the stack first.

Step 2: Match Three

When you collect three identical tiles in your bar, they automatically clear and you earn 300 ⭐ stars. This is the core mechanic — plan your moves to create matches efficiently rather than randomly picking tiles.

Step 3: Clear the Board

Your bar holds 7 slots. If all 7 slots fill up without a match, the game ends. Clear all tiles from the board before this happens to complete the stage and advance to the next level.

Power-ups

Three power-ups are available to help you through tough spots:

  • ↩️ Undo — removes the last tile you placed back to the board. Costs 50 ⭐.
  • 🔀 Shuffle — reshuffles all remaining tiles on the board into new positions. Costs 100 ⭐.
  • ⬆️ Withdraw — removes a tile from your bar and returns it to the board.

Tips for Beginners

  • • Always look for three of the same tile before picking randomly.
  • • Don't fill your bar with unmatched tiles — you'll get stuck.
  • • Use Undo immediately after a mistake, not several moves later.
  • • Claim your Daily Bonus every day for free 100 ⭐.
  • • Early levels (1–3) are easy — use them to practice without spending power-ups.
