What's New in Perfect Paw Match — May 2026

May 2026 · 4 min read

🔐 Google Login & Star Sync

You can now sign in with your Google account. Your star balance is saved to the cloud — no more losing progress when you clear your browser. Login from Settings to activate this feature.

🎵 Music Settings

Background music support has been added. Head to Settings to toggle music on or off and adjust the volume slider. BGM tracks will be added in the next update.

💳 PayPal Support Added

In addition to Stripe, you can now pay with PayPal for the Remove Ads upgrade and Zia/Zio character unlocks. Great news for players in Europe, Japan, and other regions where PayPal is preferred.

🌟 Daily Bonus System

Log in every day to claim 100 free ⭐ stars. The daily bonus resets every 24 hours. Consistent players can earn 3,000+ stars per month just from the daily bonus alone.

📱 Mobile Performance Improvements

The game board now scales properly on all screen sizes. Button layouts have been improved for mobile, and the power-up bar at the bottom is now fully visible on all devices.
